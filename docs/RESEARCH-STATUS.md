# Research Status

**Project:** Constructive Theory of Reason  
**Status:** Conceptual framework under consolidation  
**Primary language of the source discussion:** Persian

## Current working structure

The discussion has developed a five-stage framework:

1. Awareness — آگاهی
2. Cognition — شناخت
3. Knowledge — دانایی
4. Learning — یاد
5. *شهرآگاهی* — provisional English rendering: Civic/Practical Wisdom

The five stages are intended to have distinct conditions of attainment. They should not be collapsed into interchangeable labels for general intelligence or accumulated information.

## Positions explicitly reached in the discussion

- Awareness, in the current biological framing, involves recognizing novelty or difference in relation to memory; mere signal registration is not sufficient.
- Cognition concerns the development and interpretation of what has entered awareness.
- Knowledge includes the capacity to use what is known, including for prediction and judgment.
- Learning includes generalization, discovery of relationships, and the production of new understanding or solutions.
- *شهرآگاهی* concerns adaptive, goal-directed use of knowledge in a changing environment and includes the ability to explain what one knows appropriately to others.
- *شهرآگاهی* is a matter of degree; it should not be treated as a binary label.
- Within a particular dimension of a subject, the five stages are ordered. Across different dimensions, their operation may be parallel and recursive.
- Supporting processes such as memory, attention, processing, comparison, inference, evaluation, judgment, choice, and decision are not additional primary stages in the model.

## Scope boundary

The current definitions are framed around biological human cognition. The framework has not established that the same concepts or criteria apply to artificial intelligence or non-biological systems. Such an extension must not be implied by the current material.

## What this repository does not claim

At this stage, the repository does not claim:

- empirical or experimental validation;
- acceptance by a scientific or philosophical community;
- a complete account of human cognition;
- a settled English translation for every Persian term;
- that the five stages are universally linear across all cognitive dimensions;
- that the framework is a BAES foundation or has been validated by BAES.

## Publication principle

The repository should preserve the difference between:

- a position explicitly reached in the dialogue;
- a provisional formulation;
- an interpretation or editorial clarification;
- an unresolved question;
- a claim that would require external or empirical evidence.

A lack of evidence should be reported as a limitation, not filled with speculation.
